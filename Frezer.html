Algebra

Algebra is a branch of mathematics dealing with symbols and the rules for manipulating them.

Example:

x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0

This can be factored as (x+2)(x+3) = 0

Geometry

Geometry deals with shapes, sizes, and the properties of space.

Example: The area of a triangle is given by:

Area = (base × height) / 2

Calculus

Calculus studies how things change. It focuses on derivatives and integrals.

Example:

f'(x) = lim (h → 0) [f(x+h) - f(x)] / h
